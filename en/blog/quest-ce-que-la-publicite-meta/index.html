What is Meta Advertising?

Meta advertising allows you to post ads on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and other Meta Group locations to reach the right people at the right time.

Meta advertising, in brief

Meta advertising includes campaigns broadcast on Meta's platforms, including Facebook, Instagram and Messenger. It allows a company to promote an offer to a chosen audience based on its location, interests, behaviours or past interactions with the brand.

Unlike an organic publication, a sponsored ad can be quickly shown to people who don't follow you yet. This makes it a powerful lever to gain visibility, generate demand and support sales.

Where do the ads appear?

Meta campaigns can appear in the Facebook feed, Instagram stories, Reels, Messenger or the Audience Network. The format fits the lens: image, video, carousel, contact form or retargeting announcement.

  • Make your brand known at new hearings
  • Generate visits to your site or landing page
  • Receive quote requests or qualified forms
  • Relaunching those who have already shown interest

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Meta Ads and Google Ads?

Meta Ads captures attention on social networks, while Google Ads often responds to an intention already expressed in the search. Both channels can very well complement each other.

What budget does it take to start?

The right budget depends on your market, your offer and your goal. The most important thing is to plan enough volume to test audiences and creations properly.

Do we need a lot of subscribers already?

No. Meta campaigns can reach people who are not yet following your brand, which makes it an acquisition lever.
