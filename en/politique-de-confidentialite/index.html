2. Data collected

3. Purposes and legal bases

RGPD Art. 6 Consentement Legitimate interest Contrat

Management of contact requests

Legal basis: Performance of a contract / pre-contractual measures. We use your contact information to respond to your requests and prepare business proposals.

Improvement of our services

Legal basis: Legitimate interest. Navigation data analysis to optimize the user experience and performance of our site.

Advertising and remarketing

Legal basis: Consentement. We may use your data to offer you personalized ads via Meta and Google, subject to your consent.

Legal obligations

Legal basis: Legal obligation. Retention of certain data to meet our accounting and tax obligations.

4. Shelf life

  • Contact information: 3 years from last contact
  • Customer data (active contract): Contract term + 5 years
  • Navigational data: 13 months maximum (analytical cookies)
  • Advertising: 90 days (according to platform policies)
  • Accounting data: 10 years (legal obligation)

5. Data sharing

We never sell your personal data. We can share them with:

Technical contractors

  • Google (Analytics, Ads, Tag Manager) — United States, standard contractual clauses
  • Meta (Facebook Ads, Pixel) — United States, standard contractual clauses
  • Hotjar — EU, records of anonymised sessions
  • Enzuzo — Canada/EU, cookie consent management

Any transfer outside the EU shall be supervised by appropriate guarantees in accordance with the GDPR (standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions).
