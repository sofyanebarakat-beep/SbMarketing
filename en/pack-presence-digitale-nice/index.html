SEO & SEO

What does the SEO setup include?

Titles, descriptions, page structure, local keywords and technical bases for referencing.

Do you work local SEO in Nice?

Yes. We target local research related to your activity to improve your visibility with customers close to you.

Do you optimize Google Business Profile?

Yes, to improve your presence on Google Maps and in local searches.

What keywords will you target?

Relevant expressions related to your activity, your services and your geographical area, for example your business associated with Nice.

Can the SEO help get more customers?

Yes. Increased visibility of relevant research can attract more qualified visitors to your site.

How long does it take to see results?

The SEO is built over time. The first improvements may appear quickly, but lasting results require regularity.

No. We put in place solid foundations and a coherent strategy, without promising a precise position on Google.

Yes. An existing site can be reworked to better target good research, improve its structure and increase its visibility.

Will the content of my website be optimized?

Yes. We adjust the titles, descriptions and content essential to make them clearer to your visitors and better understood by Google.

Why is SEO included in this offer?

Because a beautiful site must also be found. Design attracts attention, and SEO helps good customers discover you.
