MK Multiservices is a Niçois brand that groups under one roof services that are rarely sought together: administrative procedures (driving permits, grey cards, business creation), car diagnostics, motor reprogramming and reproduction of keys for vehicles and housing.

This multi-activity positioning is a strength — but also a communication challenge. The site had to make this diversity readable without drowning the visitor, and allow each profile to quickly find what it needs, whether it is in a particular hurry or a creative entrepreneur.

Website

When so many different services are offered, clarity becomes the first selling argument. We have structured the site around three distinct universes—administrative, auto, keys—with a navigation thought that each visitor will find his answer in less than two clicks.

Each department has its own section: clear description, concrete benefits, call for immediate action. The 24/7 hotline is highlighted from the front door to capture pressing requests — often the most decisive for this type of activity.

The result: a professional site that reflects the legitimacy of MK Multiservices on Nice, reassures new visitors and simplifies the transition to action — whether for a grey card, a diagnosis or a lost key at 10pm.

A site designed to simplify procedures

Each page naturally guides the visitor to contact: quick form, visible number, clear hours and 24-hour hotline. The route is optimized to reduce friction — because when someone is looking for MK Multiservices, it often has a problem to solve now.
