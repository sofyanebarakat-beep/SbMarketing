The product

From separate tasks to one shared system

The portal gives each audience a focused workspace while keeping the underlying recruitment journey connected. Employees can reach their profile, documents, employment information, payroll and notifications. Employers can prepare and follow applications and supporting documents.

For agents and administrators, partnership positions, candidate submissions, reviews and follow-up actions live in the same product. The result is a clearer handoff between every participant and a reliable view of what needs attention next.

Four connected experiences

Useful to every role

Access and actions are adapted to each user, keeping complex workflows understandable without losing operational depth.

01 · EMPLOYEES

Everything employment-related in one place

Profiles, documents, employment information, payroll access and notifications are organized into a personal workspace.

02 · EMPLOYERS

Applications that are easier to complete and track

Employers can manage application details, supporting files, comments and the actions requested by the recruitment team.

03 · AGENTS & ADMIN

A clear operational view

Teams manage employers, applications, employees, document reviews, partnership positions and candidate submissions from dedicated controls.

Product logic

One journey, visible end to end

The interface turns a multi-party process into a sequence of clear states, actions and notifications.

1

Create

An employer starts an application or a team member opens a position.

2

Complete

Required details and documents are gathered in context.

3

Review

The recruitment team checks submissions, requests updates and records progress.

4

Follow

Notifications and dashboards keep every role aware of its next action.
