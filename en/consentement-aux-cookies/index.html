Last updated : 15 April 2026

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file deposited on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) during your visit to our site. It allows you to memorize information about your browsing to improve your experience and analyze the use of the site.

In accordance with CNIL regulations and the GDPR, some cookies require your explicit consent before they are filed. You can change your preferences at any time.

These cookies are essential to the proper functioning of the site. They cannot be disabled without affecting the use of the site.

Manage your preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time. Three ways to do this:

1. Through our consent banner

Click the button below to open the consent manager and change your choices:

Manage my cookies

2. Through your browser settings

  • Chrome : Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox : Settings → Privacy and security
  • Safari : Preferences → Privacy
  • Edge : Settings → Cookies and site permissions

3. Through platform unsubscribe tools

  • Disable Google Analytics
  • Meta advertising settings
  • Your Online Choices
