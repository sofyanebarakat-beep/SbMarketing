Background

A recruitment agency that wanted to recruit its own clients.

Outreach Recruitment is an agency specialized in the placement of professionals for fast growing companies. Despite proven expertise in the field, their online presence did not reflect the quality of their work — and incoming leads stagnated.

Their overall image has been taken in hand: a complete overhaul of the brand, a conversion-oriented website, LinkedIn content strategy and targeted advertising campaigns. The result: a pipeline of qualified candidates filled every week, and an employer brand that their customers are proud to present.
